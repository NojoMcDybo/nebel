What nebel. is for

A learning tool, not a medical device. nebel. simulates fictional people with type 1 diabetes so that relatives and friends can understand what everyday life with it feels like. The site is not intended for the diagnosis, monitoring, prediction or treatment of any disease and is not a medical device within the meaning of Regulation (EU) 2017/745.

No number from the simulation is suitable for a real therapy decision. That is the job of your diabetes care team.

The ten bodies

The model parameters of the ten people (personen-daten.js) come from the 20 virtual patients of the library py-mgipsim (Illinois Institute of Technology), version {{PYMGIPSIM_REF_SHORT}}. They are under the GNU General Public License v3 and are passed on here as a separate file under that license. Only the form was changed: ten of the twenty data sets were selected and the required fields were transferred from JSON to JavaScript. The values themselves are unchanged. Where nebel. deviates from them (sensor delay, insulin absorption), this happens only during calculation and is documented in the source code. The names, ages and looks of the people are invented.

Model

The physiology follows the extended Hovorka model by Hobbs et al. (2019), Computers & Chemical Engineering, and Siket, Rashid & Cinar (2025), Journal of Diabetes Science and Technology. The equations were implemented from the publications, not taken from the code of py-mgipsim. Fat, protein and sensor error are own additions, documented with sources in the project folder docs/ (in German).
